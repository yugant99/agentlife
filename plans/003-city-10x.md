## 1. Thesis + art direction

**Thesis:** Make the city a legible instrument — a 3-second glance tells you where work burns, who needs you, and that the city is awake.

**Art direction:** midnight harbor noir with an amber heart and jade-blue breath. Palette: obsidian ground `#141a22` / sky `#05070f`, tungsten amber windows/beam `#ff9b47–#ffcf8a`, jade archive/teal water `#6fd8b8 / #0a1e26`, violet abyss + hole smear `#241a4a / #8a9fd0`, copper Foundry / ice Observatory accents. Mood: warm work-light vs. cold abyss, one amber "needs-you" accent, everything else dark and quiet. References: Blade-Runner harbor noir (lighting report), stylized Interstellar Gargantua Doppler + photon rim (sky report, not ray-traced), TF2 silhouette-first class reads (agents report), mission-control HUDs with urgency ordering + sparklines (datamap report).

## 3. Frame budget (MacBook Air, 60 fps = 16.6 ms)

Target city view, 24 agents, DPR 1.5: JS update 2.0 ms (single `useFrame` loops, no per-frame alloc/state) | main opaque 6.5 ms (buildings 11 draws, landmarks ~12, streets ~8, water 5, workers ~15 via LOD/instancing, FX 3, tram/boats 5; total <90 draws, <350k tris) | shadow 1.2 ms (1024, ±60, casters = near workers + stations + landmarks only) | sky 0.7 ms (disk FBM masked, 2-octave field) | water 0.5 ms | bloom half-res 2.0 ms + grade/vignette/grain 0.4 ms | reserve 3.3 ms. Tram pillars 18→slim, rail tube 240→128 segs, steam spheres→billboards if over.

Tiers: **High** DPR ≤1.6, bloom half, shadow 1024, full FX/ribbons/streaks. **Med** (auto on <50 fps 2 s): DPR 1.25, bloom quarter, shadow 1024 static, ribbons on / wakes off, stars 40%. **Low**: DPR 1.0, bloom off (analytic glow only), shadows off, all-far LOD, streaks halved, `antialias:false` when composer on. **Reduced-motion/paused**: `uTime=STILL_TIME`, ribbons/wakes/twinkle/flow 0, easing frozen, beams/rings/glow static + text — fully legible still. `PerformanceMonitor`-driven, HUD `?debug=1` shows tier.
